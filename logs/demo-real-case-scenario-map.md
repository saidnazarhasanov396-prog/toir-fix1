# Demo Real-Case Scenario Map

Audit date: 2026-05-25

This map defines realistic Navoiyazot-style flows that demo seed must represent. All scenarios are intended for `dev,demo-seed` only in the implementation phase.

## A) Organization Scenario

**Goal:** show full enterprise staffing and RBAC for TOIR operations.

- **Tables:** `departments`, `hr_employees`, `users`, `roles`, `user_roles`, `brigades`, `brigade_members`, `user_certifications`, `certification_types`, `hr_timesheet_entries`
- **Data shape:**
  - 20+ departments across plant/workshop/section.
  - 20+ users and 20+ employees with linked departments.
  - 6-10 finite certification types; user certifications generated per role.
  - 15+ brigade members across 4-6 brigades.
- **Realistic examples:**
  - `CHIEF_MECHANIC` AMM user manages AMM brigade and approves WO closure.
  - `STOREKEEPER` sees warehouse notifications and stock reservations.

## B) Equipment Hierarchy Scenario

**Goal:** demonstrate plant -> sex -> line -> station -> equipment node hierarchy and dynamic passport.

- **Tables:** `equipment_nodes`, `equipment`, `equipment_types`, `equipment_passports`, `equipment_attribute_definitions`, `equipment_attribute_option_sources`, `equipment_attribute_option_items`, `equipment_attribute_values`, `equipment_attribute_required_criticality`, `equipment_attribute_value_history`, `equipment_status_history`, `equipment_meters`, `meter_readings`, `calibration_records`, `condition_readings`, `technical_documents`, `uploaded_files`, `vehicle_details`, `manufacturers`, `criticality_classes`, `service_classes`
- **Data shape:**
  - 20+ equipment nodes and 20+ assets.
  - 15+ attribute definitions, 20+ values, value history generated from updates.
  - mixed equipment statuses (ACTIVE, IN_REPAIR, RESERVE, DECOMMISSION_PENDING).
- **Realistic examples:**
  - compressor K-1 with vibration limit attribute and monthly reading trend.
  - pump passport PDF and maintenance instruction attached.

## C) Warehouse Scenario

**Goal:** demonstrate stock visibility, low-stock alerts, and movement discipline.

- **Tables:** `warehouses`, `spare_parts`, `materials`, `warehouse_stocks`, `stock_movements`, `reservations`, `warehouse_equipment_items`, `units_of_measurement`
- **Data shape:**
  - 3-5 warehouses, 20+ spare parts, 15+ materials.
  - 20+ stock balance rows with low/critical examples.
  - 20+ stock movements and 20+ reservations generated by work orders.
- **Realistic examples:**
  - seal kit below min threshold triggers notification.
  - bearing transfer from central warehouse to AMM workshop store.

## D) Defect and Repair Scenario

**Goal:** full chain from detection to closure with labor/material/safety evidence.

- **Tables:** `repair_requests`, `defects`, `defect_categories`, `defect_severities`, `failure_reasons`, `root_causes`, `work_orders`, `work_order_tasks`, `work_executions`, `labor_entries`, `repair_material_usages`, `safety_permits`, `completion_acts`, `defect_lists`, `defect_list_lines`, `sla_rules`, `escalation_events`
- **Data shape:**
  - 20+ repair requests and defects.
  - 20+ work orders with mixed statuses (`PLANNED`, `APPROVED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `CLOSED`, `POSTPONED`).
  - labor/material usage tied to completed orders.
- **Realistic examples:**
  - `RR-2026-0012` leak -> defect -> WO -> safety permit -> completion act.
  - overdue high criticality request creates escalation event.

## E) Replacement Scenario

**Goal:** show old equipment removal and new unit installation with warehouse trace.

- **Tables:** `work_orders`, `warehouse_equipment_items`, `stock_movements`, `reservations`, `equipment`, `technical_documents`, `uploaded_files`
- **Flow:**
  1. Replacement-type work order created for failed unit.
  2. New warehouse equipment item selected/reserved.
  3. Old equipment moved back to warehouse (`RETURN` / `TRANSFER_TO_WH`).
  4. New equipment installed and status switched to active.
- **Realistic examples:**
  - old pump `NAV-AMM-PMP-03` returned to repair stock.
  - replacement unit serial and installation act document attached.

## F) PPR Scenario

**Goal:** show preventive maintenance lifecycle from regulation to task status outcomes.

- **Tables:** `maintenance_regulations`, `maintenance_regulation_attribute_conditions`, `maintenance_templates`, `maintenance_operations`, `ppr_plans`, `ppr_tasks`, `maintenance_kpis`, `planned_shutdowns`
- **Data shape:**
  - 15+ regulations/templates and 20+ operations/tasks.
  - ppr task statuses include `PLANNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `POSTPONED`, `OVERDUE`.
- **Realistic examples:**
  - quarterly compressor vibration route generated only for matching passport attributes.
  - postponed task has explicit reason and new planned date.

## G) Budget / Cost / Contractor Scenario

**Goal:** tie maintenance execution to finance and external contractor control.

- **Tables:** `maintenance_budgets`, `budget_lines`, `cost_categories`, `actual_costs`, `financial_approval_rules`, `actual_cost_review_route_overrides`, `contractors`, `contractor_contracts`, `contractor_works`, `procurement_requests`, `procurement_request_lines`, `approval_requests`, `approval_steps`
- **Data shape:**
  - 15+ budget lines, 20+ actual costs.
  - procurement requests generated from low stock and planned works.
  - approval chains with approved/rejected/escalated steps.
- **Realistic examples:**
  - WO material overrun triggers review-route override before final approval.
  - contractor balancing work tied to contract and accepted cost posting.

## H) Documents / Audit / Notifications Scenario

**Goal:** prove traceability of actions, files, and user alerts.

- **Tables:** `uploaded_files`, `technical_documents`, `file_assets`, `audit_logs`, `notifications`, `knowledge_articles`, `integration_endpoints`, `integration_sync_logs`, `webhook_subscriptions`, `webhook_event_log`
- **Data shape:**
  - 20+ uploaded/document records and 20+ notifications/audit records.
  - integration sync + webhook event logs can remain excluded from deterministic 15-row target (runtime technical logs).
- **Realistic examples:**
  - low-stock and overdue-PPR notifications sent to responsible roles.
  - audit entries for status changes and approval decisions.

## Cross-Scenario Coverage Checklist

- Organization + RBAC: covered in A and reused in all scenarios.
- Equipment hierarchy and passport: covered in B and consumed by D/F.
- Warehouse + replacement: covered in C and E.
- Defect to repair completion chain: covered in D.
- PPR lifecycle and overdue/postpone states: covered in F.
- Budget/cost/contractor/approval chain: covered in G.
- Files/audit/notifications/integration traces: covered in H.
